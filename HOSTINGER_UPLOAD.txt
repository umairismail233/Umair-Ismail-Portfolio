HOW TO UPLOAD TO HOSTINGER:
1. Log in to your Hostinger hPanel.
2. Go to Websites -> Manage -> File Manager (for your domain).
3. Open the public_html folder.
4. Upload Umair-Ismail.zip directly into public_html.
5. Right-click Umair-Ismail.zip and choose "Extract".
6. Ensure index.html, styles.css, script.js, Umair-Ismail-Resume.pdf, and favicon.svg are directly inside public_html (not nested in an extra subfolder).
7. Visit your domain over HTTPS. Everything is 100% active and live!
